Image 1 shows two panels from a mouse liver study. Panel A has haematoxylin and eosin stained liver sections of five groups, and each section carries the name of its group above it. Panel B gives the mean serum aspartate aminotransferase and the mean serum alanine aminotransferase of the same five groups, where the group names sit on the horizontal axis and the legend tells which bar belongs to which enzyme.

Find how many groups meet both of the conditions below, and report that number.

The first condition is about the section. The liver section of the group shows no "macrovesicular steatosis". A section counts as steatotic when large round clear vacuoles fill most of the hepatocytes across the whole field. A clear space which is a vessel lumen or a bile duct is not steatosis, so a section which holds only those spaces still counts as free of steatosis.

The second condition is about the serum. For that same group the ratio of the mean aspartate aminotransferase to the mean alanine aminotransferase is greater than 2. Read each bar to the nearest 10 U/L, take the top of the bar and not the top of the error bar, then treat the numbers you read as exact.

Report your final answer as a single integer, without units and without any other text.
