## What this kills

The trap was signal versus artifact: vessel lumens in the two clean sections standing in for fat
droplets. The model did not go near it. Obvious steatosis against clean parenchyma is not a
discrimination a current model gets wrong, so histology of this kind is not a lever on its own.

Coarse reading is not a lever either. The whole reason the answer was a count rather than a
measurement was that the axis cannot support three significant figures. The model simply read
the bars accurately anyway and the coarse axis cost it nothing.

## Checker findings on the revised prompt, for the next one

Three majors came back and all three are worth fixing in whatever replaces this:

1. SPECIFICITY_MISSING. Asking for "the two values you read" with no precision or rounding rule.
2. READING_RULE_MISSING. Saying to judge the bars by eye against the ticks does not say what

   vacuoles filling more than half of each cell, or as vacuoles appearing in more than half of
   the cells.

Findings 1 and 2 pull against the figure itself. The axis has no minor gridlines, so any stated
rounding step is finer than the figure supports, and saying nothing is a finding. A published
figure with coarse ticks cannot satisfy that checker. The next design needs a figure whose
axis carries real minor gridlines.
